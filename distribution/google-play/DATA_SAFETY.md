# Google Play declarations - prepared answers

These answers describe the store build produced with `--dart-define=STORE_BUILD=true`.

- Data collected or shared: No. App data remains on device and is not transmitted by the app.
- Data encrypted in transit: Not applicable; the app does not transmit learner data.
- Account creation: None.
- Account deletion URL: Not applicable.
- Ads: No.
- App access: No login or restricted access instructions.
- Sensitive permissions: None requested by the main Android manifest.
- Financial features: None in the store build. The voluntary HesabPay panel is excluded.
- External content: An optional button opens the public Code With Safi YouTube channel outside the app.
- Primary category: Education.
- Suggested target audience: Ages 13 and over. The owner must confirm this selection in Play Console.
- Content rating: Complete the IARC questionnaire in Play Console using the actual app behavior; do not infer a rating in advance.
- Privacy policy: Host `distribution/privacy-policy.html` at a stable public HTTPS URL and enter that exact URL in Play Console.
